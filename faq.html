Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about this sample online store.

Need a quick answer?

These answers describe the intended demo experience, not a live commercial service.

Contact us
Is Shopora a real store?

No. Shopora is a fictional front-end website created for a student assignment.

Can I place a real order?

No. The cart and checkout are static demonstrations. They do not connect to inventory, a server, or payment provider.

How do I find products?

Use the Shop page, category links, New Arrivals, or Best Sellers from the main navigation.

Does the website work on mobile?

The responsive CSS adjusts layouts and shows a collapsible navigation menu on smaller screens.

How can I contact support?

Use the Contact Us form. It displays a demo confirmation without sending information.

Can I return an item?

The sample policy is explained on the Shipping & Returns page; no real returns are processed.
